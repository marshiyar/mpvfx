# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in MpVFX spaces must be respectful, constructive, and safe. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, disclosure of private
information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect different backgrounds, abilities, and levels of experience.
- Keep bug reports factual and avoid including private media or credentials.
- Accept moderation decisions and disengage when asked.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, issue warnings, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately to repository
maintainers. Do not use a public issue when a report contains sensitive personal information.

Maintainers will limit disclosure to the people needed to review and resolve a report. Retaliation
against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.
